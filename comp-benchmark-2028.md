# Compensation Benchmark — target season Dec 2027, graduation 2028

> Built 1 September 2026. Every number below is web-sourced and cited. Refresh before the Dec 2027 placement season; comp data ages badly.
> Context: Integrated M.Tech Geological Technology, IIT Roorkee, 2023-2028. Currently ₹50,000/month at Finrep (≈ ₹6 LPA equivalent). ~15 months to placement season.

## 1. The four markets are not one market

The single most important fact: **these are four different hiring systems with different entry conditions**, and three of them are close to shut for an India-based fresh graduate applying to a *product* title.

Manthan's own `jobs-nightly` sweep is the proof, not a guess: **76 ATS boards, 11,111 roles indexed, 7 early-career product roles.** The scarcity is structural, not a coverage problem (see memory `project_jobs_nightly_ats_ceiling`).

| Market | Open to an India-based 2028 grad? | Reality |
|---|---|---|
| **India campus** | **Yes, fully** | The only market where a fresh grad is the *intended* candidate. Highest predictable ceiling |
| **India off-campus** | Yes, but thin | APM/PM-intern roles exist; comp below campus band |
| **US remote** | **Rarely for PM** | US startups hire India-based contractors mainly in engineering, DevOps, AI/ML, support. PM is a proximity role; almost nobody hires a first-job remote PM across 10 time zones |
| **Europe remote** | **Rarely, and lower net than it looks** | Entry PM €50-65K gross, taxed 40%+ with high cost of living. Relocation-based, not remote-from-India |
| **Dubai / UAE** | Possible but relocation, not remote | Zero income tax is the real edge. Sponsorship for a fresh-grad PM is uncommon; the roles that sponsor skew senior |

## 2. The numbers

### India campus (the primary target)

- **IIT Roorkee 2025-26:** CSE average ₹34.05 LPA, median ₹30.5 LPA. ECE ₹30.81 LPA, Electrical ₹28.36 LPA, **Civil ₹16.62 LPA**. Highest domestic ≈ ₹1.5 crore, highest international ≈ ₹2.05 crore. ([Shiksha](https://www.shiksha.com/college/iit-roorkee-indian-institute-of-technology-3057/placement), [Collegedunia](https://collegedunia.com/university/25992-iit-roorkee-indian-institute-of-technology-iitr-roorkee/placement))
- **Branch matters.** Geological Technology sits with the non-circuital branches, whose averages run roughly half of CSE. **The 28-34 LPA band is reachable only through open-to-all product / analytics / tech roles, not through the branch's default recruiters.** This is the single biggest structural fact in the plan.
- **InMobi ~28 LPA:** plausible and consistent with the top of the IIT campus band for product/analyst roles. Not verified against a primary source; treat as directionally right, not confirmed.

### India, market rate (off-campus, entry PM)

- **Entry-level PM, India: ₹11.4L - ₹20.7L.** ([Levels.fyi](https://www.levels.fyi/t/product-manager/levels/entry-level/locations/india), updated Mar 2026)
- **Entry-level PM, Bengaluru: ₹14.7L - ₹25.9L.** ([Levels.fyi](https://www.levels.fyi/t/product-manager/levels/entry-level/locations/greater-bengaluru), updated Jun 2026)
- **Google APM1, India: ₹30.7 LPA** (median PM package across levels ₹1.12 crore). ([Levels.fyi](https://www.levels.fyi/companies/google/salaries/product-manager/locations/india))

**Read:** campus at 28 LPA is roughly the *top decile* of the entry-PM market in India, and it beats the off-campus median by ~2x. Campus is not a fallback. It is the best-paying accessible door.

### US remote, from India

- India-based contractors for US companies: **$3,000-8,000/month** (₹32-85 LPA), typically contractor terms, no benefits, paid via Deel/Wise/Payoneer. Engineers in Pune/Bengaluru: **$4,000-9,000/month**. ([letsmoveglobally](https://letsmoveglobally.com/blog/working-remotely-india-us-company-salary-2026), [tech-lein](https://tech-lein.com/remote-tech-jobs-from-india-2026/))
- A handful of companies pay **location-independent**: Circle pays US-benchmarked salary plus equity regardless of location; DuckDuckGo uses region-banded transparent pay. GitLab, Automattic, Zapier, Deel hire across 65-100+ countries. ([remotivated](https://remotivated.com/best-remote-companies/work-model/remote-first))
- **Caveat that matters:** these figures are for experienced engineers. Almost none of it is fresh-grad PM.

### Forward Deployed Engineer (the direction, not the entry point)

- **Entry FDE: $130-180K total comp** (₹1.15-1.6 crore). Mid $180-260K. Senior $260-350K+. ([recruitingfromscratch](https://www.recruitingfromscratch.com/blog/forward-deployed-engineer-salary-in-2026-full-guide))
- Frontier labs (OpenAI, Anthropic) pay 2.0-3.5x Palantir at the same level, with the gap almost entirely in equity; equity is now 55-70% of comp at the top. ([Perspective AI 2026 FDE comp report](https://getperspective.ai/blog/2026-forward-deployed-engineering-compensation-report-1200-fdes))
- **These are US-located, work-authorised roles.** Not a graduation target from India. They are a *year 2-4* target after a first job builds the record.

### Europe

- **Berlin APM: ~€54K**, entry PM from €60K; Berlin PM median €76K in 2026 including sign-on (€69K cash-only). **Amsterdam entry PM: €44.5K - €65.8K**, median total €56.1K. ([CareerCheck](https://www.careercheck.io/blog/product-manager-salary-berlin-2026), [Levels.fyi Amsterdam](https://www.levels.fyi/t/product-manager/levels/entry-level/locations/greater-amsterdam-area))
- €60K gross ≈ ₹62 LPA on paper. **After 40%+ tax and Berlin/Amsterdam rent, absolute savings are lower than a ₹30 LPA package in Bengaluru.** Europe is a lifestyle and optionality move, not a comp move.

### Dubai / UAE

- Senior PM in Dubai: **AED 300K - 504K** (≈ ₹70L - ₹118L). Entry roles with sponsorship advertise **$40K-70K**; graduate programs (e.g. Mastercard Launch) **$80K-110K**. ([Levels.fyi Dubai](https://www.levels.fyi/t/product-manager/locations/dubai-are), [zerotaxjobs](https://zerotaxjobs.com/salaries/product-manager/dubai))
- **Zero personal income tax** is the genuine edge: AED 300K net ≈ what ₹1.2 crore gross would net in India.
- Glassdoor Dubai figures in search results conflate monthly and annual; **do not quote Glassdoor Dubai numbers without re-verifying the period.**

## 3. What this means

1. **The ₹28 LPA reference point is a campus number, and campus is the right primary target.** It is the only market where being a fresh grad is an advantage rather than a disqualification.
2. **The branch is the constraint, not the ambition.** Geological Technology does not carry a 28-34 LPA default. Every rupee above the branch average has to come from being independently credible for an open-to-all product/AI role.
3. **International remote is not a fresh-grad comp lever.** It becomes one after a first job, and it opens fastest through the FDE / AI-engineer door, not the PM door.
4. **Dubai is the only geography where the headline number and the take-home number both improve.** Europe does not clear the bar on net savings. The US clears it enormously but is closed without authorisation.
5. **The real lever is category scarcity.** Nothing about applying harder moves the number. What moves it is being a demonstrably top-decile candidate in a scarce category. Manthan's category — AI product person who ships production code, runs evals, and owns analytics end to end — is scarce and appreciating.
