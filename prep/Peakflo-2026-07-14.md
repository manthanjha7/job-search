### Product Sense (very likely, tailored to their domain)
**"How would you improve Peakflo?" / "Design an agentic workflow for [invoice processing / vendor onboarding / expense approvals]."**
Use **CIRCLES**: Comprehend → Identify user → Report needs → Cut/prioritize → List solutions → Evaluate → Summarize.
- **Persona:** AP clerk / finance controller at a mid-market B2B (their sweet spot).
- **Pain points to name:** invoice data-entry from messy PDFs; 3-way match (PO ↔ invoice ↔ receipt); chasing approvals; exception handling; reconciliation across ERPs (NetSuite/Xero/QuickBooks).
- **Agentic angle (score points here):** where does the agent act autonomously vs. where does a human stay in the loop? **Confidence thresholds, escalation on low confidence, and an audit trail** — this is exactly the friction/eval lens you already apply to Fina. Tie it to their LLM-as-a-Judge eval need.
- **Metrics:** workflow completion rate, % straight-through (no human touch), exception rate, time-to-pay, accuracy vs. human baseline.

### Analytical / Metrics
- **"How would you measure success of an agentic invoice workflow?"**
  North star: **% invoices processed straight-through with correct outcome.** Guardrails: error/exception rate, human-intervention rate, time-to-completion, cost per invoice. Counter-metric: don't optimize throughput by silently mis-paying — accuracy is the constraint.
- **"How would you eval the agent's quality?"** → **This is your home turf.** LLM-as-a-Judge rubric with explicit criteria (did it extract fields correctly? right GL code? right approver?), a labeled gold set, precision/recall on exceptions, and human spot-audit of judge agreement. Reference your FINA eval + session-monitor friction-pattern work concretely.
- **Conflicting-metric tradeoff** (2026 pattern): automation rate ↑ vs. accuracy ↓ — how do you set the confidence threshold? Talk expected cost of a wrong payment vs. cost of human review.
