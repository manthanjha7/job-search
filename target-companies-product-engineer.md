# Target Companies — hiring at graduation (2028)

> Rebuilt 1 September 2026 around the correct frame: **companies that hire new graduates**, for a 2028 exit. Target role: Product Engineer / AI Engineer / SDE, desk-based IC.
> Two doors, and they are different lists: **campus placements Dec 2027** and **off-campus new-grad hiring through 2028**.
> Companion docs: `comp-benchmark-2028.md` · `FDE/foundations-roadmap.md` · `tracker.md`

## Correction to the previous version of this list

The earlier draft was built around a Summer 2027 internship and led with remote-first companies. That was the wrong frame for a graduation target, because **PostHog, Supabase, Cal.com, Circle and most remote-first companies do not run new-grad hiring at all.** They hire experienced engineers. They are a year 2-4 destination, not a 2028 one, and they have been moved down accordingly.

## Reference point: the anchor is low

InMobi at ~₹28 LPA sits **mid-table** on this list, not at the top. Stripe's India office pays a Tier-1 IIT B.Tech fresher about **₹65L in first-year total comp** (₹29L base + ₹23L RSU + ₹3L bonus + ₹4.35L joining). That is 2.3x the anchor. Aim higher than the number you heard.

## Tier E — The ceiling that is probably not yours

Quant and HFT are the true top of Indian campus comp: **₹2-3 crore** at Jane Street, Optiver, Graviton, Tower Research; domestic ₹80L-1.5cr at Goldman, Tower, DE Shaw. Others: IMC, Da Vinci, NK Securities, Squarepoint, Quadeye, AlphaGrep, WorldQuant.

**These land almost exclusively in CSE plus quant/HFT roles.** With a Geological Technology branch, no DSA base and 15 months, this is not a realistic target. Listed so it is consciously ruled out rather than quietly chased.

## Tier F — Remote-first global, year 2-4

**PostHog** (Product Engineer title, ~$190K, no offices) · **Supabase** (37 work-from-anywhere roles) · **GitLab** (65+ countries) · **Automattic** (90+ countries) · Cal.com · Zapier · Deel · Hugging Face · Grafana · **Circle** (US-benchmarked pay regardless of location).

Excellent destinations, wrong timing. They hire on demonstrated production experience, not on graduation. Reach them **from** Tier A/B/C after two years. Open-source contribution is the legitimate side door and can start now.

## The two doors

**Door 1 — Campus, Dec 2027.** IIT-R saw **160+ recruiters** last season, and **SDE was the single most-recruited profile**. Google, Microsoft, Amazon and Meta are the branch-agnostic ones. **Blocked on one unresolved fact: which roles a Geological Technology student may sit for.** One email to the placement cell resolves it. Until then everything in this paragraph is speculation.

**Door 2 — Off-campus new-grad, 2028.** Tier B's Emerging Talent-style programs, Tier C startups, and Tier D companies that hire on GitHub. **This door does not care about branch.** It cares about whether there is public evidence he ships.

Manthan should work both. Door 1 is higher and more predictable if it is open. Door 2 is entirely within his control and is the one his 2026-27 work compounds into.

## What this list demands of the next 15 months

1. **Resolve branch eligibility.** One email. It decides how much of Door 1 exists.
2. **Get the engineering title at Finrep.** Every company above reads "PM intern" and "Product Engineer at an AI fintech" completely differently.
3. **Public artifacts.** Tier B, C, D and F all hire on evidence. There is currently none outside Finrep's Slack.
4. **AI/ML plus data engineering is the most-demanded GCC skill set and he already has it.** The gap is proof, not capability.
