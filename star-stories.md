> Full STAR (Situation, Task, Action, Result) narratives extracted from resume bullets.
> Use these in interviews, outreach, and cover letters. Each story is tagged with common interview questions it answers.

---

## What is STAR?

- **Situation:** The context -- what was happening, what was the problem?
- **Task:** What were YOU specifically asked/expected to do?
- **Action:** What did YOU do? (Be specific -- tools, decisions, steps)
- **Result:** What was the measurable outcome?

**Tips from people who've done PM interviews:**
- Keep each story to 90 seconds when spoken
- Always quantify the result (numbers, percentages, time saved)
- Use "I" not "we" -- interviewers want YOUR contribution
- Have 6-8 stories ready that cover different themes (analytics, user research, GTM, leadership, conflict, failure)

## Finrep Stories

### Story 1: Building Analytics from Zero
**Resume bullet:** Built Finrep's analytics foundation, instrumenting Mixpanel taxonomy across 7+ product surfaces...

**Situation:** Finrep had no structured analytics. The team was making product decisions based on gut feel and anecdotal feedback. There was no way to measure feature adoption, user flows, or where users were dropping off.

**Task:** I was tasked with building the entire analytics infrastructure from scratch -- deciding what to track, how to structure events, and setting up dashboards the team could use daily.

**Action:**
- Designed a Mixpanel event taxonomy covering 7+ product surfaces (Fina, Research Hub, Grid, etc.)
- Instrumented events across the platform with consistent naming conventions
- Set up Metabase dashboards for funnel analysis and key metrics
- Conducted regular session replay reviews to understand qualitative user behavior
- Identified a pattern of session abandonment at a specific point in the workflow

**Result:** Shipped a re-engagement trigger based on the abandonment pattern, which reduced session abandonment. The analytics infrastructure became the foundation for all subsequent product decisions at Finrep.

**Use for questions about:**
- "Tell me about a time you built something from scratch"
- "How do you use data to drive product decisions?"
- "Describe a time you identified a problem and solved it"
- "What's your experience with product analytics?"
